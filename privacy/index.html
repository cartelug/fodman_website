Privacy Policy

How Fodman International Ltd collects, uses, and protects your information.

Last updated: 20 July 2026

Fodman International Ltd ("Fodman", "we", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect when you use our website or contact us, and how we handle it.

Information we collect

  • Information you give us — such as your name, organisation, email, phone number, and the details of an enquiry, quotation request, or loan application you submit.
  • Technical information — basic, non-identifying data your browser sends when you visit, such as device type and pages viewed.

How we use your information

  • To respond to enquiries, prepare quotations, and deliver the services you request.
  • To assess and administer loan and lending applications where you apply for finance.
  • To communicate with you about your request and our services.
  • To meet our legal, regulatory, and record-keeping obligations.

Sharing your information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with our staff and trusted partners where necessary to deliver a service you have requested, or where required by law.

WhatsApp and email enquiries

When you contact us through WhatsApp or email, your message is handled by the relevant platform under its own terms in addition to this policy.

Data security

We take reasonable steps to keep your information secure and to limit access to those who need it to serve you.

Your choices

You may ask us to access, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you by contacting us at the details below.
