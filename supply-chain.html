Built for Complex Operations

From specialized sourcing to cross-border relief convoys.

The Supply Chain & Logistics Division of Fodman International is engineered to handle complex, high-stakes supply operations across East Africa. Operating out of primary hubs in Kampala and Juba, we provide seamless connectivity and dependable service delivery in both stable and challenging operational contexts.

From sourcing highly specialized materials to navigating cross-border customs for relief convoys, our infrastructure is designed around accuracy, compliance, and speed.
