Logistics & Supplies

Built for Complex Operations

From specialized sourcing to cross-border relief convoys.

The Supply Chain & Logistics Division of Fodman International is engineered to handle complex, high-stakes supply operations across East Africa. Operating out of primary hubs in Kampala and Juba, we provide seamless connectivity and dependable service delivery in both stable and challenging operational contexts.

From sourcing highly specialized materials to navigating cross-border customs for relief convoys, our infrastructure is designed around accuracy, compliance, and speed.

Core Capabilities

Six integrated service lines.

Each capability is delivered to international procurement and compliance standards.

Integrated Freight & Transport

End-to-end management of land and air freight for commercial and relief goods, ensuring optimal routing and security across borders.

Warehousing & Distribution

Secure inventory management, regional consolidation hubs, and rapid last-mile delivery with full tracking.

Institutional Procurement

Specialized sourcing strategies for development programs, NGOs, and government agencies matching UN procurement standards.

Compliance & Customs

Expert navigation of cross-border regulations, tax exemptions, and clearance documentation to prevent border delays.

Water & Energy Supplies

Procurement and transport of heavy engineering materials, water treatment chemicals, and solar infrastructure.

Medical Logistics

Cold-chain management and compliance-driven transport for pharmaceuticals and emergency health kits.
