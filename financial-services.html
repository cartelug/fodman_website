Questions

Common questions

Short answers, taken from how we work. Ask our team if yours is not here.

What should I ask before I borrow?
Ask about the requirements, the total amount to repay, the repayment schedule, and what happens if a payment is late. A good lender welcomes these questions.
How much should I borrow?
Borrow only what you can comfortably repay.
What does Fodman Financial Services offer?
Money lending and loan services.
How does Fodman approach lending?
Our value is ethics: principled, just and honourable in all dealings. We grow well with others and take an explicit stance against dishonest activity.
