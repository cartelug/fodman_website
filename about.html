History & Growth · Since 2013

From localized supplier to regional strategic partner.

Founded in 2013, Fodman International has emerged as a critical partner for institutional operations in Uganda and South Sudan. We identified a need for service providers who understand both complex logistics and the high-level compliance required to navigate the regional regulatory landscape.

Over the past 10+ years, we have grown from a localized supplier into a regional strategic partner — trusted by governments, non-governmental organizations, and development agencies across East Africa.
