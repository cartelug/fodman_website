Who we are

Founded by Makerere University professionals

Registered in 2013. A supplies-and-logistics company in Uganda and South Sudan, with tailor-made general supplies for businesses in all industries, plus educational and professional consultancy for government and NGOs.

Meet the teamRead the company profile

Our story

A regional company with professional roots

Fodman International was founded by a team of highly trained professionals from Makerere University, Kampala, and registered in 2013 with the Uganda Registrar of Companies as a Limited Liability Company.

We operate from Kampala and Juba, with a focus on Uganda, South Sudan and the wider Great Lakes region, backed by international partnerships.

Where we are going

One of the biggest in East Africa, and in time the wider continent

We intend to grow into one of the biggest supplies-and-consultancy companies in the East African region, and in time to reach the wider African continent.

Our commitment

Honest growth, shared with our clients

Honesty and integrity

We strongly believe that the best way to grow is to grow well with others. Our aim is not to take unfair advantage over any person or entity.

We shall always endeavour to have a balanced business environment that gives us and our clients the opportunity to grow and leverage their worth, while benefiting each other.

Our promise to clients

Fodman International is committed to providing quality and professional services to satisfy the diverse needs of our clients. Our esteemed clients can be confident that, in doing business with us, we will return value on their investment in our services.

Balanced growth

A business environment where we and our clients can both grow and benefit.

No unfair advantage

We prevent, discourage and avoid dishonest activity.

Long-term relationships

Finest quality service, so clients stay with us for the long run.

Whenever a customer has a challenge in our line of interest, that is an opportunity, and we shall be there to deliver.

Corporate social responsibility

Accountable to five groups, not one

We treat CSR as an ethical duty, not a bolt-on. Our actions must benefit the whole of society.

  • Customers
  • Suppliers
  • Employees
  • Shareholders
  • Communities

We balance economic growth against the welfare of society and the environment we operate in, and we are accountable to our customers, suppliers, employees, shareholders and communities.

Consumers and job-seekers increasingly judge businesses on this. The impact of Fodman should be felt by the people wherever we run our business, because it is the best we can do to cause positive change.
