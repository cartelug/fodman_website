Leadership and team

Led by a founder with 20+ years’ experience

A board of directors and a management team that run supplies, operations, finance, HR, research, training, public relations and marketing every day.

Send an enquiry

Managing Director and founder

Francis Odada

Director since 2010, with more than 20 years in business administration, project management, research and statistics, M&E, programme design and management, organisational management, and supplies and logistics management. He previously held progressive senior positions with a number of companies and NGOs, reducing costs and achieving operational efficiencies.

  • MA Management (M&E), Makerere University
  • BSc Statistics, Makerere University
  • PGD Project Planning and Management, Uganda Management Institute

Board of directors

Four directors who oversee the company

Administration, finance and operations, HR, and research and training.

Gwendolyne Kyampaire

Assistant Managing Director

Oversees general administration, chairs high-level meetings with clients, stakeholders and partners, and handles urgent payments and financial matters. Acts as Managing Director in his absence.

Hilda Achikuet Marial

Director, Finance and Operations

Worked as a Finance Director with various companies and organisations before joining Fodman. Supervises procurement, daily business transactions and relations with Fodman’s bankers, and lobbies and negotiates contracts on the company’s behalf.

Richard Ebwolu

Director, Administration and HR

Oversees the HR department. Plans and coordinates staff activities such as recruitment, interviews and supervision in line with performance, targets and contract renewals, and runs a sustainable HR programme.

WO

Wilfred Opobo

Director, Research and Training

Oversees the research and training department. Plans and coordinates activities such as identifying organisations in need of training, and designs training and research programmes.

Management team

The people who run the work every day

Five managers and officers carry operations, HR, research, training, public relations and marketing.

John Duku Jojos

Administration and HR Manager

Reports to the head of department. Plans and assists in coordinating staff matters such as salaries and allowances, interviews, recruitment and performance appraisals, and helps maintain a sustainable HR programme aligned with Fodman’s strategy.

Robinah Anyago

Operations Manager

Reports to the operations director and plans and coordinates all company operations. The commercial and operations department under her supports the company with relevant, effective and targeted business ideas for its products and services.

Norah Ajoje

Research and Training Manager

Reports to the head of department. Assists in planning and coordinating training and research activities, including supervision of ongoing training sessions.

Catherine Anyait

Public Relations Officer

Creates and maintains good relationships between Fodman, clients, partners and stakeholders, and plans and coordinates marketing, advertising and sales promotions.

Evelyne Nyakato

Marketing Officer

Assists in planning and coordinating marketing and sales activities, such as identifying potential clients in need of services and procurement, and spotting partnerships and business opportunities.
