Common Questions

Loan process FAQ.

How long does assessment take?

Assessment timing depends on the request and whether all required documentation has been received. A lending officer confirms the expected timeline for each application.

What determines my interest rate?

Rate and tenure depend on the loan product, amount, and your risk profile — these are confirmed during assessment and set out clearly in your loan agreement before you sign.

Do I need a guarantor or collateral?

Requirements depend on the product, requested amount and assessment. A lending officer will confirm exactly what applies before you proceed.

Can I repay early?

Yes. Early repayment terms are set out in your loan agreement — ask your lending officer for the specifics before you sign.

What if I'm based in South Sudan?

Our Juba office serves South Sudan directly, with the same process and requirements as our Uganda desk, adapted to local documentation where needed.
