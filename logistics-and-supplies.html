Questions

Common questions

Short answers, taken from how we work. Ask our team if yours is not here.

What goods do you supply?
Seven categories: electronics and IT equipment; office and domestic furniture; stationery and equipment; branded and promotional materials; packed and fresh foods; building and construction materials; and water equipment and treatment chemicals. We also shape general-supplies services to the client and the industry.
Do you handle clearing and forwarding?
Yes. We clear and forward goods without the hassle and unnecessary delays, and give logistical support in a seamless way.
How does delivery work?
Delivery is door to door, on a 24-hour basis, on tailor-made transport. Goods stay safe, and in the right hands, from the point of purchase to the client’s premises.
Who do you supply?
Businesses in all industries, including schools, corporates, government and NGOs, in Uganda, South Sudan and the wider Great Lakes region.
How do I ask for a quote?
Use the enquiry form on this page, or call +256 775 858 924. Tell us what you need, how much, where it should go and when you need it.
